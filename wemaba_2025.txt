Financial and Operational Highlights Wema Bank delivered an exceptional financial performance in FY 2025, characterised by aggressive growth and balance sheet strength. Key financial metrics include: Gross Earnings: Surged by 52.79% to ₦660.62 billion (up from ₦432.4 billion in FY 2024). Profit Before Tax (PBT): Increased by 116.42% to ₦221.85 billion. Profit After Tax (PAT): Grew by 125.36% to ₦194.46 billion. Total Assets: Expanded by 41.48% to reach ₦5.07 trillion. Customer Deposits: Rose by 30.34% to ₦3.29 trillion, driven by retail and corporate digital activities. Loans and Advances: Increased by 44.68% to ₦1.74 trillion, supported by effective risk management (NPL ratio closed at 4.90%). Operational Efficiency: The Cost-to-Income Ratio (CIR) improved significantly, reducing to 47.26% from 56.35% in the previous year, despite rising operating costs. Market Opportunity and Competitive Position Wema Bank solidified its competitive positioning by emerging as the best-performing banking stock in 2025. The bank's robust business risk profile, sustainable profitability, and strong liquidity have been recognised by major rating agencies, resulting in upgraded national ratings from Fitch (A-), Agusto & Co. (A-), and GCR (BBB+). Furthermore, the bank's digital dominance through ALAT provides a significant competitive advantage in capturing Nigeria's unbanked and underbanked populations, particularly among SMEs and retail clients. Growth Strategies and Initiatives The bank is currently executing the 2024–2026 phase of its strategic plan, aiming to transition into a Systemically Important Bank (SIB). Key growth initiatives include expanding its digital innovation portfolio (ALAT, ALATPay, SARA for Women) and driving sustainable finance. Wema Bank is heavily invested in ESG initiatives, boasting a Bbb+ ESG rating, powering 36 branches with solar energy, and providing over ₦62 billion in uncollateralised loans to women-led MSMEs. Management Insights and Future Outlook Management maintains a highly optimistic outlook for the near future, projecting continued aggressive growth. For the FY 2026 budget guidance, the bank aspires to achieve a Profit Before Tax of ₦300 billion, Total Assets of ₦6.26 trillion, and Customer Deposits of ₦4.29 trillion. The leadership team is focused on maintaining a high Return on Average Equity (ROAE), targeting 34.27% in 2026, while continuing to leverage digital transformation and operational efficiency to drive shareholder value. Inflationary Pressures: While trending downwards in early 2026, inflation remains a risk due to structural supply constraints and energy price shocks. Exchange Rate Volatility: Despite improved reserves, FX volatility risks persist depending on sustained inflows and diaspora remittances. Monetary Policy Shifts: A cautious, data-driven approach by the Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN), with the monetary policy rate sitting high at 26.5%. Regulatory Stress Tests: Ongoing pressure on Nigerian lenders to meet CBN's Risk-Based Capital (RBC) stress test deadlines and navigate intense regulatory scrutiny.
