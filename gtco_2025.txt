(GTCO) Financial Year 2025 Summary Robust Financial Performance in a Dynamic Market Guaranty Trust Holding Company Plc. (GTCO) has announced its audited financial results for the year ended 31 December 2025. The Group demonstrated resilience with stable gross earnings of ₦2.15 trillion , a marginal increase from ₦2.15 trillion in the previous year. However, reflecting a challenging operating environment, profit before tax stood at ₦1.23 trillion , compared to ₦1.27 trillion in 2024. Profit for the year attributable to equity holders was ₦853.5 billion . This translates to a Basic Earnings Per Share (EPS) of 25.43 Kobo , down from 35.44 Kobo in 2024. Key Operational Highlights The 2025 financial year was a landmark period for GTCO, highlighted by its successful dual listing on the London Stock Exchange (LSE) on 9 July 2025. This strategic move makes GTCO the first financial institution in West Africa to achieve this, enhancing its global visibility and access to international capital markets. Operationally, the company expanded its digital footprint by enhancing its suite of digital banking solutions, including GTWorld, USSD, and Internet Banking. A significant step to support businesses was the launch of a Naira Card for global transactions and the implementation of zero processing fees on all its Point of Sale (POS) terminals. In line with its commitment to sustainability, GTCO advanced its transition to solar energy across its branch network and launched the innovative "Waste-for-Gas Initiative" to promote clean energy and waste management. Market Insights and Competitive Positioning GTCO navigated a complex market characterised by an evolving regulatory landscape and persistent macroeconomic challenges. The management noted that while the credit landscape is improving, risks remain due to interest rate volatility and inflationary pressures. The company's strategy is firmly focused on becoming a dominant ecosystem of financial services across Africa, leveraging technology and maintaining a moderate risk appetite to ensure sustainable growth and stability. Management remains committed to its core philosophy: "To enhance shareholders’ value by creating and maintaining a culture of intelligent risk-taking." This guiding principle underscores the Group's strategic decisions, balancing growth opportunities with prudent risk management to deliver long-term value to all stakeholders. Investment Outlook and Strategic Initiatives Looking ahead, GTCO is focused on strengthening its capital base and expanding its market presence. The company successfully raised ₦504 billion in capital for its flagship subsidiary, GTBank Nigeria, to support its growth trajectory. Future strategy will continue to centre on dominating priority sectors across the continent, driven by technological innovation and operational efficiency. The Group has also set clear sustainability targets, aiming to reduce greenhouse gas emissions by 20% and non-recyclable waste by 30% over the next five years. Risk Factors and Challenges The Group has identified several key risks that could impact future performance: Credit Risk: The report highlights heightened counterparty and investment risks stemming from ongoing macroeconomic uncertainties. Market Risk: The company is actively monitoring the impact of changing cash reserve requirements and interest rate fluctuations on its market position. Regulatory Risk: The financial services sector faces a dynamic regulatory environment, which requires continuous adaptation and compliance to mitigate potential penalties. Dividend and Shareholder Returns Reflecting confidence in its financial position and commitment to shareholder value, the Board of Directors has recommended a significant increase in shareholder returns. A final dividend of ₦11.76k per share has been proposed. This, combined with the interim dividend of ₦1.00 per share, brings the total dividend for the 2025 financial year to ₦12.76k per share , a substantial increase from the ₦8.03k per share paid in 2024. The presentation outlines the company's successful evolution from a pure-play banking franchise into a comprehensive financial ecosystem encompassing Banking, Fund Management, Pension Management, and Payments (Fintech). GTCO's core strategic objective is to shape the future of finance in Africa by making end-to-end financial services simple, accessible, and transformative. The Group aims to empower individuals and businesses by leveraging robust technology, fostering strategic partnerships, and maintaining strong governance principles. Financial and Operational Highlights GTCO delivered a highly resilient financial performance in FY 2025, underpinned by strong growth in core earnings: Profitability: Profit Before Tax (PBT) stood at a robust ₦1.231 trillion. Although this represents a slight 2.8% year-on-year decrease due to the non-recurrence of massive FX revaluation gains seen in 2024, core earnings were exceptionally strong. Interest income and fee income grew by 23.2% and 25.88% respectively. Balance Sheet Strength: Total Assets grew by 20.0% to reach ₦17.76 trillion. Total Deposits increased by 23.5% to ₦12.87 trillion, while Gross Loans and Advances grew by 10.6% to ₦3.229 trillion. Efficiency and Returns: The Group maintained a highly efficient Cost-to-Income Ratio (CIR) of 27.86%. Return on Average Equity (ROAE) closed at 28.28%, and Return on Average Assets (ROAA) stood at 5.32%. Capital and Asset Quality: The Capital Adequacy Ratio (CAR) closed at an impressive 43.82%, providing significant headroom for future expansion. The Non-Performing Loan (NPL) ratio improved slightly to 4.97%, with a strong coverage ratio of 106.91%. Market Opportunity and Competitive Position Operating across 11 countries, including a presence in the UK, GTCO maintains a strong Pan-African footprint. The Group holds best-in-class credit ratings (Fitch: B, S&P: B-) within its peer group. GTCO is uniquely positioned to capture market share through its interconnected ecosystem, cross-selling services from its dominant retail banking franchise to its fast-growing non-banking subsidiaries. Notably, its payment subsidiary, HabariPay, recorded a 195.4% year-on-year growth in Total Processing Value (TPV), highlighting the massive opportunity in Africa's digital payments landscape. Growth Strategies and Initiatives The Group's strategic priorities are focused on diversifying earnings and positioning for transformational growth. Key initiatives include unlocking ecosystem collaborations across its four business verticals to drive customer retention and value. GTCO is heavily investing in technology to enhance operational efficiency, cybersecurity, and digital banking capabilities. Furthermore, the Group is employing a digital-first approach to scale its non-banking subsidiaries, driving rapid customer acquisition in the Pension and Fund Management sectors. Management Insights Management remains highly optimistic about sustaining strong profitability anchored on core earnings growth, effectively offsetting the normalisation of exchange rates. Looking ahead, the Group has provided robust guidance for FY 2026, projecting a PBT of ₦1.400 trillion, deposit growth of 40%, and loan growth of 25%. Management is focused on maintaining a healthy, well-diversified balance sheet while navigating the macroeconomic environment with disciplined cost management and prudent risk practices. Risks and Challenges Macroeconomic Headwinds: Sustained inflationary pressures and exchange rate volatility across operating regions continue to impact operating expenses and the translation of subsidiary earnings. Rising Regulatory Costs: Increased regulatory charges, specifically AMCON levies and Deposit Insurance Premiums, have driven up overall operating costs. Sector-Specific Credit Risks: While the overall NPL ratio is healthy, specific sectors such as Construction (97.6% NPL) and Education (42.3% NPL) exhibited high impairment rates, requiring disciplined credit risk management and strategic deleveraging.
